ChromaRipple Privacy & Support

For ChromaRipple on iPhone and iPad (com.chromaripple.game).

Privacy

ChromaRipple does not require an online account and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The game saves optional player nicknames, challenge progress, high scores, and preferences on your device. The developer does not receive this game data.

Apple processes payment for the optional Plus upgrade. The app uses StoreKit to verify whether Plus was purchased. ChromaRipple does not receive your payment details. Apple Privacy Policy

To remove a local profile, open Player Profiles, tap the profile’s options and choose Delete Profile. To remove all the game’s local data, delete the app from your device.

Support

Tap How to Play for game rules. If you already bought Plus, open Plus → Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account.

For privacy or support questions, email privacy@floweye.app. We use information you choose to include in your message to respond to your request.

幻彩涟漪隐私与支持

本说明适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的《幻彩涟漪》（com.chromaripple.game）。

游戏无需注册网络账户，也不使用广告、分析或追踪 SDK。可选昵称、闯关进度、最高分和设置保存在本机，开发者不会收到这些游戏数据。

可选的 Plus 买断由 Apple 处理。游戏通过 StoreKit 验证购买权益，不接收付款资料。Apple 隐私政策

如需删除单个档案，请在档案的选项中选择「删除档案」。如需移除全部本机记录，可卸载游戏。

游戏规则见首页「玩法说明」。已经购买 Plus 的玩家可使用原 Apple 账户，在「Plus → 恢复购买」找回权益。

隐私和支持问题可发至 privacy@floweye.app。我们会使用你主动提供的信息回复你的请求。
