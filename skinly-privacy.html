Skinly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Skinly ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Skinly handles your information.

1. On-Device Storage by Default

Your skin profile, daily routine check-ins, skin logs, product shelf, and photos are stored locally on your iOS device by default. Skinly does not require account registration and does not maintain a user database of your personal identity.

2. Optional AI Analysis

When you actively request AI skin observation, product ingredient recognition, or personalized skincare insights, the selected photo or anonymized skincare summary is transmitted over HTTPS to our AI inference provider solely to return your skincare analysis. Data is not used for advertising, cross-app tracking, or user profiling.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Skinly Pro offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) and a one-time Lifetime purchase processed securely by Apple App Store via StoreKit 2. Payment and subscription management are governed by Apple's Standard Terms of Use (EULA).

4. Non-Medical Disclaimer

Skinly is a daily skincare tracking and ingredient education tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, prescriptions, or treatment advice.

5. Your Control & Data Deletion

You can export your skincare report or permanently delete all local photos, logs, products, check-ins, and insights anytime from Profile → Delete All Photos & Logs.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email qubc11@gmail.com or visit our Support Page.
