Need help with Skinly (Daily Skincare Log & Routine)? We're here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where are my photos stored? All skin photos and logs are saved locally on your device. You can delete them anytime under Profile → Delete All Photos & Logs.
  • How do I manage or cancel Skinly Pro subscriptions? Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and can be managed or canceled anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions at least 24 hours before renewal. You can also tap Restore Purchases inside Profile or the Skinly Pro sheet.
  • How do I switch languages or themes? Open the Profile tab to switch between English, Simplified Chinese, or System Language, and choose from Rose Mist, Forest Sage, or Cream Oat themes.
  • Does Skinly provide medical advice? No. Skinly is a daily skincare habit tracker and ingredient education tool only.
